Windows 11 installieren

Benötigt:

Windows ISO Datei

Rufus

USB-Stick, mindestens 8GB (alle Daten darauf werden gelöscht!)

Vor Installation alle wichtigen Daten extern absichern

Bootfähigen USB-Stick erstellen

Rufus öffnen

  1. Im Menü "Laufwerk" den USB-Stick auswählen

  2. Unter "Startart" die heruntergeladene Windows 11 ISO Datei auswählen

  3. Rufus konfiguriert automatisch die nötigen Formatierungseinstellungen

  4. "Start"

  5. Der Prozess kann einige Minuten dauern. Sobald der Status "Fertig" angezeigt wird, kann das Programm geschlossen werden

Installation

Alle externen Speicher bis auf den bootfähigen USB-Stick entfernen

PC neustarten, beim Neustart F11 oder F12 (modellabhängig) gedrückt lassen, um ins Bootmenü zu gelangen

  1. "Ein Gerät verwenden"

  2. Bootfähigen USB Stick auswählen

  3. Sprache und Tastatur auswählen → Weiter

  4. "Jetzt installieren"

  5. „Ich habe keinen Product Key“

  6. Die Ziel-Festplatte auswählen (meist „Nicht zugewiesener Speicherplatz“)

  7. „Benutzerdefiniert: nur Windows installieren“ wählen

Nach der Installation mit MAS aktivieren

A Guide to Baking an Existential Crisis Cake

A surprisingly bitter dessert for the discerning palate.

The Ingredients

This is not your grandmother's recipe, unless your grandmother was a nihilist philosopher with a sweet tooth. Procuring these ingredients requires more than a trip to the supermarket. It requires introspection.

Tangible Goods

Begin with the basics. You will need 2 cups of all-purpose dread, 1 cup of melted ennui, and 3 eggs of mild-to-moderate disappointment. For leavening, a tablespoon of cosmic indifference is essential. Do not substitute with baking soda; the philosophical flavor profile will be completely off.

Intangible Concepts

Next, fold in one lingering memory of a childhood dream you have long since abandoned. Add a dash of the sudden realization that you are just a collection of star-dust on a meaningless trajectory. Sweeten to taste with the faint, persistent ringing in your ears that only you can hear.

The Preparation

In a large bowl, whisk the dread and ennui until they form stiff, grey peaks. In a separate bowl, beat the eggs of disappointment while staring blankly into the middle distance. Combine the two mixtures with a spatula of resignation. It is important not to over-mix. The batter should have the lumpy consistency of a Tuesday afternoon.

Gently incorporate the cosmic indifference and your chosen intangibles. You will know the batter is ready when it sighs audibly. Pour it into a poorly-greased pan, for what is baking without the Sisyphean struggle of a cake that will not release?

The Bake

Preheat your oven to a vaguely unsettling temperature. Bake for what feels like an eternity, but is probably around 35 minutes. Do not check on the cake. A watched pot never boils, and a watched cake will only judge your life choices. The cake is done when a toothpick inserted into the center comes out questioning its own purpose.

The Presentation

Let the cake cool on a wire rack of shattered expectations. There is no need for frosting, as the cake is already layered with a thick glaze of irony. Serve in complete silence to a room full of people checking their phones. Pairs well with lukewarm tap water and the crushing weight of your own consciousness. Enjoy?
